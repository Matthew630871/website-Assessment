Chalets Booking And Capacity Rules

Important Booking Infomation

  • Chalets are strictly acessible to club members only.
  • All chalet bookinjgs must run from Friday to Sunday.
  • Shared accomodation accommodates between 6 and 40 people per chalet.
  • Ski directly to and from your chalet door on the mountain

1. kākāpo

Capacity: Max 10 Adults | Max 30 Children

  • Largest of all chalets
  • Large kitchen, dining, and living area
  • Great amenities for large groups
  • Accommodates up to 40 people

2. Pūkeko

Capacity: Max 6 Adults | Max 15 Children

  • Great for large gorups/familys wanting privacy
  • Capacity limits ensure a comfortable stay
  • Shared living facilities
  • Direct mountain ski access

3. Kererū

Capacity: Max 2 Adults | Max 4 Children

  • Great option for individual families
  • 2 bedrooms
  • Perfectr for families with 2-4 children
  • Seperate living area
